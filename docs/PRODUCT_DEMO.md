# 90-second product demo

## Audience

Owner or receptionist of an independent Indian clinic who currently manages patient enquiries and appointments manually through WhatsApp.

## Storyboard

| Time | Scene | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Patient sends a WhatsApp appointment question | “Every missed message can become a missed appointment.” |
| 8–20s | Helpa answers an approved FAQ | “Helpa responds instantly using information your clinic controls.” |
| 20–35s | Doctor availability and open slots appear | “It checks real availability before offering a time.” |
| 35–48s | Patient confirms; appointment is created | “One reply turns the conversation into a confirmed visit.” |
| 48–60s | Confirmation and reminder are sent | “Patients receive clear confirmations and reminders on WhatsApp.” |
| 60–72s | Staff inbox and takeover control | “Your team sees every conversation and can take over at any time.” |
| 72–82s | OPD slip and follow-up workflow | “Visit documents and follow-ups stay connected to the patient journey.” |
| 82–90s | Outcome screen and trial CTA | “Faster responses, fewer manual follow-ups, and a calmer front desk.” |

## Required screenshots

Capture real product states at 1440×900, with seeded fictional patient data:

1. Clinic dashboard overview.
2. WhatsApp inbox with an appointment enquiry.
3. Doctor availability and slot selection.
4. Confirmed appointment record.
5. Automated confirmation/reminder preview.
6. Staff takeover and conversation assignment.
7. OPD slip or follow-up workflow.

Do not use production patient data. Blur phone numbers and identifiers if a staging copy contains imported records.

## Recording acceptance criteria

- 85–95 seconds at normal playback speed.
- Captions included and readable without audio.
- No compliance claims, fake ratings, or unverified outcome figures.
- All data is fictional and visibly representative.
- Export 1080p H.264 plus a caption file.
